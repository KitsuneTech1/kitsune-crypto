Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about speeds, fees, consensus, and privacy:

1. How fast are cross-chain deposits and withdrawals?

EVM Layer-2 stablecoins (Base & Arbitrum USDC) and Solana complete in 2 to 3 seconds. Bitcoin Lightning payments settle in under 1 second. Monero atomic swaps complete in approximately 2 minutes.

2. What are the network and bridge fees?

The base network transaction fee is fixed at 0.05 KPL. Fast bridge swaps carry a transparent 0.15% liquidity fee (minimum $0.50) with zero hidden slippage.

3. How does KPL protect privacy without trusted setups?

Every transfer derives one-time stealth addresses ($P = H(rA)G + B$) and 16-member ring signatures (CLSAG) over Curve25519. Zero-knowledge range proofs utilize Bulletproofs+, which require zero trusted setup parameters.

4. Is there a centralized custodian holding bridged funds?

No. Bridge operations utilize non-custodial hash-time locked contracts (HTLC) and cryptographic adaptor signatures. If a counterparty or relayer becomes unresponsive, timelocks guarantee automatic refund of your principal.

5. How does consensus eliminate 51% attacks and reorganizations?

Consensus employs single-slot Byzantine Fault Tolerance with Quorum Certificates signed by $\ge 2f + 1$ validators. Unlike Proof-of-Work, chain reorganizations and forks are mathematically impossible under Byzantine threshold $f < n/3$.
